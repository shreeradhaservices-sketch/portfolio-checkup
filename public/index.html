  • KFintech (mfs.kfintech.com) — for KFin-serviced folios
  • CDSL (cdslindia.com) — combined demat + mutual fund statement
  • NSDL (nsdl.co.in) — if your demat account is with NSDL

Most platforms will email you a password-protected PDF within a few minutes — the password is usually your PAN. A full step-by-step guide will be linked here soon.

    Things worth knowing

    Goals and risk profile matter more than scheme selection

    Investment outcomes are generally shaped less by picking historically well-performing schemes and more by whether your investments are mapped to clearly defined goals (retirement, a child's education, a home purchase) and a risk profile suited to your personal situation and time horizon.

    A qualified advisor can help you think through how your goals, time horizon, and risk appetite should shape your investment choices — this report only shows you what you currently hold, not what you should do about it.

    What this report does not cover: NPS, EPF/PPF, insurance policies, bank fixed deposits, physical gold, real estate, or any holding not part of a mutual fund/demat CAS. This report is not a complete picture of your net worth.
